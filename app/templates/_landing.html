SubGuard

AI reads the fine print. PayPal enforces the outcome.

SubGuard reads the fine print on free trials, and uses PayPal to stop the charge you didn't agree to.

Before you pay, it tells you the real last day to cancel. Before that day, it asks if you want to keep the trial. If you say no, it cancels the subscription in PayPal before the first charge.

Try it yourself: open the checkout ↗

She wants Penwright Studio, a writing app: "{{ L.trial_days }}-day free trial, $0 today"

The fine print says:

  1. After {{ L.trial_days }} days it costs {{ L.intro }} a month, then {{ L.step_up }} a month from month {{ L.intro_months + 1 }}.

  3. There is no cancel button: email, or phone on weekdays only.

Without SubGuard: {{ L.year_one }} in year one. With SubGuard: {{ L.zero }}.

  1. 1

    Before you pay

    It reads the terms, checks them against the PayPal plan, and gives you the real last day to cancel.

  2. 2

    Before the deadline

    It asks you once: keep the trial or cancel it? You decide.

  3. 3

    At the deadline

    If you said cancel, it cancels in PayPal before the first charge, and drafts the email the terms ask for.
